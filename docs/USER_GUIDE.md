# Pharma-Khaato Quick User Guide

## First setup

1. Open Pharma-Khaato.
2. Enter the pharmacy details.
3. Create the Administrator account.
4. Sign in.
5. Add other staff later from **Settings → Staff & Access**.

## Main menu

- **New Sale** — sell products and take payment.
- **Customers** — add or find a customer and see their balance.
- **Customer Credit** — see outstanding customer credit and record payments.
- **Stock & Products** — add products, receive stock, and review stock/expiry alerts.
- **Shift & Cash** — start or close the cash shift and record cash added/removed.
- **Reports** — review sales, low stock, expiring items, and activity.
- **Settings** — change pharmacy settings, staff access, backups, and support details.

## New Sale

1. Start your cash shift from **Shift & Cash** if it is not already open.
2. Scan a barcode or type the product name/formula.
3. Add the product to the sale.
4. Select a customer only when needed. Walk-in cash/card sales do not require registration.
5. Choose how the customer will pay.
6. Select **Complete Sale**.

### Useful keys

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| **F1** | Find a product |
| **F2** | Choose a customer |
| **F3** | Apply discount |
| **F4** | Choose payment |
| **F5** | Hold the current sale |
| **F6** | Resume a held sale |
| **F10 / F12** | Complete the sale |
| **Delete** | Remove the selected cart item |
| **+ / -** | Increase or decrease selected quantity |

### Payment choices

- **Cash** — enter the cash received to see change due.
- **Card / Easypaisa / JazzCash** — cash received is not required.
- **Customer Credit** — requires a registered customer and must remain within the configured credit limit.

### Discounts

A large discount can require approval from a manager or administrator. This protects the counter from accidental or unauthorized discounts.

## Customers & Customer Credit

Use **Customers** to add/search customers and check the amount due. Use **Customer Credit** to see credit records and record a full or partial payment.

## Stock & Products

Use **Products** to add or edit product information. Use **Add Stock** when new stock arrives and enter its quantity, batch number, and expiry date.

When a product has more than one valid batch, the system automatically sells stock with the earliest expiry first. Expired stock cannot be sold.

## Shift & Cash

At the start of the counter shift:

1. Open **Shift & Cash**.
2. Select **Start Cash Shift**.
3. Enter the starting cash in the drawer.

During the shift, use **Add / Remove Cash** when cash is placed into or taken out of the drawer for a reason other than a normal sale.

At closing, select **Count Cash & Close Shift**, enter the counted cash, and review any difference.

## Reports

Use Reports for:

- sales totals and number of sales,
- cash and customer-credit totals,
- low-stock products,
- items expiring soon,
- activity history.

## Settings

- **General** — tax, customer credit limit, receipt text, theme, and interface size.
- **Staff & Access** — add staff, set their role, and reset passwords.
- **System & Backup** — check system health and create backups. Technical details are available only when needed for support.

## Keyboard navigation

Use **Alt+1** through **Alt+7** to move between the main screens. All important actions also remain available as visible buttons, so shortcuts are optional.
