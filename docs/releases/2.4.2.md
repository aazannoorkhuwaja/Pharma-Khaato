# Pharma Khaato 2.4.2

This update makes everyday screens easier to read and use on pharmacy computers.

- Settings fields and sidebar buttons keep their space at larger text sizes. Scroll to reach additional controls.
- Supplier balances display complete amounts. Invoice entry gives quantity, cost and expiry more room.
- Customer suggestions show a name with phone and area underneath, without internal account numbers.
- Help text follows light/dark mode. The theme choice stays in sync with the sidebar switch.
- Sale unit controls fit their rows, including after changing text size.
- Expiry Alerts clearly marks expired stock.
- Search handles missing contact fields in older customer records; stale search results cannot replace newer text.
- Changing invoice medicine clears old unit choices until the new ones load.

Back up your shop before updating. Install over the existing version; your shop records remain in their separate data folder.

Windows and Ubuntu/Linux Mint installers are built by GitHub Actions. A successful automated build does not certify your physical printer. Follow [the shop trial guide](../SHOP_TRIAL.md) before switching real customer work to this version. Windows signing and physical printer acceptance must be checked before public stable distribution.
