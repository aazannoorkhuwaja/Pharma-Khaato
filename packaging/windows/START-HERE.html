Start here

Pharma Khaato makes pharmacy bills and keeps stock and dues. Billing on one computer works without internet.

Use a spare computer and practice records first. Keep using your current system for real customers until you have checked the app, printer and scanner.

Install and open

  1. Double-click the file ending in Setup-Windows-x64.exe. Follow the steps and keep the desktop shortcut selected.
  2. Open Pharma Khaato from your desktop.
  3. Create your owner account and enter your shop details. Keep the password.

Use Windows 10 version 1809 or later, or Windows 11, on a 64-bit Intel/AMD computer. Use the same Windows account each day. If Windows blocks the installer, ask the person who supplied it to help; keep Windows security enabled.

Your first practice bill

  1. Add a practice medicine at Rs. 5 per tablet. Add 100 tablets with a future expiry. Keep tax and discount at zero for this example.
  2. Start a shift with Rs. 1,000 opening cash.
  3. Sell 3 tablets. Leave the customer as Walk-in. Enter Rs. 100 received.
  4. The bill should be Rs. 15, the change Rs. 85, and remaining stock 97 tablets.

Daily use

  • Scan or search for a medicine. Check the strength and whether you are selling a tablet, strip or box.
  • Cash customers do not need to give personal details.
  • For credit, select the correct customer. A name is enough to create an account; phone and CNIC are optional.
  • Choose Complete Sale once. If you are unsure whether it saved, choose Check This Bill before taking payment again.
  • If printing fails after the sale is saved, reprint that bill. Do not make a second sale.
  • Use Hold Sale to save unfinished work. Count cash before closing your shift.

Keep a backup

Save a copy of your shop records on another physical device. Ask for help practising a restore. Do not delete the shop data folder to fix a problem.

Updates and help

Open About Pharma Khaato to see the builders, check for updates or turn automatic checks off. An update notice shows its fixes. Choose Later to keep working; nothing installs automatically.

Tell the person testing this app which task was difficult, what happened, and the app version. Hide customer details in screenshots and never share passwords.
