# Product
Pharma Khaato supports offline pharmacy checkout, batch inventory, customer dues, returns and shared counters. The owner configures tax and customer discounts. A cashier can use configured prices during checkout. Prescription verification and batch eligibility apply independently.

Cash received at checkout must be an exact number of paisa. A cashier can correct a rejected amount using the same checkout request. A retry of a completed sale uses its recorded payment and receipt.

The [site protocol](../docs/PHARMACY_SITE_ACCEPTANCE.md) defines the operating scenarios a pharmacy must verify on its equipment.
